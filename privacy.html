Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

File Utility uses the Google Drive API to create, list, read, update and delete application-authorized files and folders. It requests the drive.file permission rather than general access to all Google Drive files.

File contents and archive metadata are encrypted locally before storage in Google Drive. Retrieval and decryption occur locally. Google processes stored encrypted data and associated Drive metadata as the storage and authorization provider.

Authorization tokens and encryption keys are stored locally in permission-protected files. They are not published on this website. Google user data is used only to provide the file storage and retrieval functions described here. It is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with other parties apart from the storage provider.

Stored files are retained until removed by the authorized user. Access may be revoked in Google Account settings; revocation does not itself delete stored files.

This site has no login form, added analytics or advertising. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process visitor request information under GitHub's privacy practices. Questions about data access or deletion may be directed to the support contact shown on the Google consent screen.
