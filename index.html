Backups

A private backup utility operated by its owner for personal use. It uses Restic and rclone to save encrypted backups to the owner's Google Drive and restore them when needed.

Google Drive access is limited to files created by this application or explicitly authorized for it, using the drive.file permission. This is not a public backup service or a signup page.

The owner manages the application and its Google authorization through their own server and Google Account.
